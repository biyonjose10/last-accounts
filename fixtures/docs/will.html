LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT
OF
RUTH CALLOWAY

I, Ruth Calloway, a resident of Tampa, Florida, being of sound mind, declare this to be my Last Will and Testament and revoke all wills and codicils I have made before.

ARTICLE I. MY FAMILY

My children are my daughter, Dana Calloway, and my son, Tom Calloway. My niece is Aoife Calloway of Cork, Ireland.

ARTICLE II. PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVE

I name my daughter, Dana Calloway, as Personal Representative of my estate, to serve without bond.

ARTICLE III. DEBTS AND EXPENSES

I direct my Personal Representative to pay my lawful debts, the expenses of my funeral and the costs of administering my estate, in the order the law requires.

ARTICLE IV. GIFT OF MONEY

I give the sum of Two Thousand Dollars ($2,000.00) to my niece, Aoife Calloway, of Cork, Ireland.

ARTICLE V. RESIDUE OF MY ESTATE

I give all the rest, residue and remainder of my estate, in equal shares, to my daughter, Dana Calloway, and my son, Tom Calloway.

Signed at Tampa, Florida, on the 12th day of June, 2019, in the presence of the two witnesses named below, who signed at my request and in the presence of each other.

Ruth Calloway
Ruth Calloway, Testator
Harlan Pennick
Witness, Tampa, Florida
June Abernethy
Witness, Tampa, Florida
