# Live rules and submission checklist

Checked 6 Nov 2026 against https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com (home, `/rules`, `/resources`). Re-check the day before submitting.

## Judging
- Stage one: pass/fail on fit with the theme.
- Stage two: five criteria, equal weight: Technological Implementation, Design, Potential Impact, Innovation/Idea, Presentation.
- Submissions close Thu 12 Nov 2026, 12:00 PT (Fri 13 Nov, 01:30 IST). Judging 1–15 Dec. Winners about 21 Dec.
- One grand prize and one sponsor prize at most per project.
